About

My Journey to Financial Freedom

I spent fifteen years inside global agency networks.
The kind of career that looked right from the outside.
First London, then Dubai, later Singapore.
High salary, impressive job titles. WPP, Omnicom, Facebook.

Then the pandemic came.
I decided to make the switch. Start working for myself.
