Why Most People Never Reach FIRE

It’s not that the maths don’t work. It’s that nobody ever showed them the right ones.

The Default

Work until 65. Save what’s left. Hope the pension holds. Retire in the expensive country you can’t afford to leave.

The Alternative

Build income that doesn’t need you. Move where the maths work. Retire when you decide — not when the system says so.

One is a plan. The other is a default. Only one of them is yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your call.

Can I really change my career?

Yes — and it matters far less how old you are or what your background is than you might think. The world of work has changed: remote roles, freelancing, consulting, and small online businesses have opened paths that simply didn’t exist a decade ago. Career change rarely means starting from zero — most people repackage the skills they already have into work that pays better, fits their life better, or both. Whether that’s going remote in your current field, becoming self-employed, or starting a business you can run with ease, we map the options against your situation and build a realistic transition plan — step by step.

Is it mandatory to relocate?

Not necessarily. Relocation is one of the most powerful tools for reaching Coast-FIRE faster — it lets you dramatically lower your cost of living while often reducing your tax burden, which means your savings rate accelerates significantly. But it’s not a hard requirement. We can build a plan around your existing situation, your family setup, and any constraints that make relocation off the table. If you stay put, we work harder on the income and investment side to make the numbers work where you are. Every plan is built around your life — not a template.

I have very little savings — can I still work towards Coast-FIRE?

Yes, but the starting point looks different. Before we focus on investments, we need to build the engine: increase your income, cut your biggest costs, and get your savings rate to a level where real progress is actually possible. That might mean a side income stream, a strategic career move, or a location change that immediately improves your numbers. Coast-FIRE is a journey, not a starting line. The maths always starts somewhere — what matters is getting the direction right from day one and building momentum from there.

Will the coaching sessions be recorded?

Yes. Every session is recorded and the link is shared with you within 24 hours of the call. You can revisit any part of the conversation whenever you need to — whether that’s to refresh your memory on a specific number, re-read a recommendation, or share the recording with a partner or spouse who wasn’t on the call.

Will the session notes be shared?

Yes. After each session you receive a written summary covering what we discussed, the decisions made, the numbers we worked through, and the concrete next steps. You’ll never have to rely on memory alone. The notes are yours to keep, reference, and act on at your own pace — they’re designed to be practical, not decorative.

What if I have questions after the session?

You can reach out any time between sessions and I’ll do my best to respond within 1–2 business days. The goal is that you never feel stuck waiting for the next call to move forward. Clarity shouldn’t have to wait. For more in-depth follow-up, we can always use part of the next session to dig deeper into whatever came up since we last spoke.
