Frequently Asked Questions

The objections worth raising before you book a call.

What exactly is a Plan B?

Not a vague backup, and not a resignation letter in a drawer. It’s three concrete things built in parallel: an income that doesn’t depend on your employer, investments that compound whether you’re working or not, and a location option you’ve actually costed. Individually each one is useful. Together they turn “what if something happens” into “here’s exactly what I’d do” — which is a very different thing to carry around with you.

What if I have no idea what mine should be?

That’s the most common starting point, and it’s exactly what the first two sessions are for. Your Plan B is usually hiding in what you already do — skills someone would pay for directly, a niche you understand better than the market does, or savings that aren’t working nearly hard enough. We find it, pressure-test it against your real hours and your real risk appetite, and rule out the routes that don’t fit. Most people need a few focused hours a week, not a second full-time job.

Can I really change how I earn at my age?

Yes — and it matters far less how old you are or what your background is than you might think. Remote roles, consulting, fractional work and small online businesses have opened routes that didn’t exist a decade ago. It rarely means starting from zero: most people repackage skills they already have into something that pays independently of their employer. We map the options against your real situation and build a transition you can actually execute.

I don’t have much to invest. Is this still for me?

Yes. Investing isn’t only for the rich — that’s one of the more expensive myths going. An extra $100 a month, invested consistently, snowballs faster than most people expect, and the habit matters more than the starting figure. If the amount is genuinely too small to move the needle today, we work on the income side first, so there’s something to invest.

I’m not planning to move abroad.

That’s fine — location is a lever you may never pull. But knowing precisely what it’s worth changes every other number in your plan: how much you need, how long it takes, and how much runway you really have. We cost it out so it’s a genuine option sitting in your back pocket, not a fantasy or a last resort.

Will the coaching sessions be recorded?

Yes. Every session is recorded and the link is shared with you within 24 hours of the call. You can revisit any part of the conversation whenever you need to — whether that’s to refresh your memory on a specific number, re-read a recommendation, or share the recording with a partner or spouse who wasn’t on the call.

Will the session notes be shared?

Yes. After each session you receive a written summary covering what we discussed, the decisions made, the numbers we worked through, and the concrete next steps. You’ll never have to rely on memory alone. The notes are yours to keep, reference, and act on at your own pace — they’re designed to be practical, not decorative.

What if I have questions after the session?

You can reach out any time between sessions and I’ll do my best to respond within 1–2 business days. The goal is that you never feel stuck waiting for the next call to move forward. For anything more involved, we can use part of the next session to dig into whatever came up since we last spoke.
